> Force the Windows 10 version 2004 update to install now with Microsoft's Update Assistant instead of waiting for the staggered rollout.

> **Note:** Windows 10 mainstream support ends October 2025. If you're on Windows 10, consider upgrading to Windows 11 rather than installing older feature updates.

Are you still waiting for your Windows 10 computer to receive the 2004 update? Eager to play with WSL2 like I was? **Here is how to force the windows 10 2004 update.**

Microsoft is staggering the over-the-air update as they usually do with new major releases. However if you are tired of hitting ‘check for updates’ in the windows 10 update settings, there is a way to press the issue:

Visit the official Windows 10 download site allows you to download and install the in-place upgrade tool. This will handle the download and installation of the 2004 update. 

One tip for ensuring a seamless upgrade is to disable or suspend BitLocker or any other 3rd party full disk encryption systems.
